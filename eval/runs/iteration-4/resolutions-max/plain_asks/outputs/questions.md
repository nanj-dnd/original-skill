4. **Ending:** Should the post end with practical advice on making resolutions stick, or should it argue something else, like skipping resolutions entirely or simply explain the failure without fixing it?

5. **Research:** Do you want a stat or two (like the often-quoted "most resolutions are abandoned by February"), or should it stay purely personal and opinion-based?

6. **Anything to match or avoid:** Is there a post of yours I should match in voice, or any clichés or topics you'd rather I stay away from?
