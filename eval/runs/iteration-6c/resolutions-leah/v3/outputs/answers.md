1d: Not a willpower problem. Most resolution advice assumes you work 9 to 5. "Same time every day," "morning routine": that doesn't exist if you're on rotating 12s, or doing nights in a warehouse, or up with a newborn. A lot of resolutions die because the method was never built for your life in the first place.

2a: Keep it practical, but no SMART-goal stuff. Give tips that actually work on a schedule that won't hold still, mainly tying the habit to an event instead of a clock time.

3: a and b, honestly. I tried a 6am gym habit three years in a row and it was dead by February every time, because the rotation always flipped me to nights. What finally worked was "the gym is the first thing I do after any shift ends, day or night." No clock involved. I've kept that up for 14 months now.

4d: Mostly other nurses and healthcare people, since they find me through my Instagram. Anyone on shifts or with a schedule that isn't normal, really. They've heard the generic advice already and it didn't fit them.

5: Mostly practical and warm, a little wry. Not rah-rah. And please don't use the word "simply" anywhere. Nothing about this is simple when your week is three nights and then two days.
