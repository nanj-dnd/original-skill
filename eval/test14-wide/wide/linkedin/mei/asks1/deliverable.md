One year managing a supermarket team of 18, after five years on the floor. Here's the one thing that made the biggest difference.

I changed when the rota goes up. It used to be posted a week ahead. Now it's three.

That's it. No new system, no app, no all-staff meeting.

What happened:

Last-minute sick calls went from about six a month to two. Turns out a lot of "sick" was really "I only found out on Thursday that I'm working Saturday and I already said yes to something."

Two people who'd told me they were thinking of leaving stayed. Both mentioned the rota when I asked why.

Fewer gaps meant fewer frantic calls to cover shifts, which meant I actually had time to do the planning that kept the gaps from appearing. It feeds itself.

Five years on the floor and I never realised how much of the chaos came down to one spreadsheet being published two weeks late.
