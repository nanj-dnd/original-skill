## What everyone gets

A pasted prompt produces something like: **"Our AI-powered route optimisation can cut your fuel costs and delivery times by up to 20%. Worth a 15-minute call?"** The choices behind it:
- **Angle:** generic cost savings on fuel and miles, with no reason given for writing to this company now.
- **Opening:** a question about their pain ("Are rising fuel costs eating into your margins?").
- **Proof:** an unsourced industry stat or "companies like yours," with no real customer named.
- **Structure:** hook, then three bullet features (real-time rerouting, easy integration, dashboards), then a call ask.
- **Tone:** upbeat salesy-professional, signed by a generic rep.

## A few questions first

Reply with something like `1b 2a 3c 4a`, and add the free-text details where an option asks for them.

**1. Proof.** The default leans on a vague "up to 20%" figure. What real result can the email stand on?
- a) A customer result with a number, for example "Cut a 40-van fleet's daily miles by 12% in 6 weeks." Type yours, and say whether the customer can be named.
- b) A pilot or trial result. Type the figure, even if it's small or early.
- c) No customer results yet. Lead with one specific thing the product does instead. Type what it is.
- d) Other: type it.

**2. Hook.** The default opens with a generic fuel-cost pain question. Why write to *this* company?
- a) Something specific about them, such as a new depot, an expansion, hiring drivers, a new contract or a LinkedIn post. Type it.
- b) A problem you solve that typical tools don't, such as mid-day re-routing, driver hours rules, mixed fleets or working with their existing TMS. Type which one.
- c) Keep the cost and fuel savings hook, done well.
- d) Other: type it.

**3. The ask.** The default asks for a 15-minute call. What do you want them to do?
- a) A 15-minute call.
- b) A free analysis of one week of their routes, showing what they'd have saved.
- c) Just reply "yes" and you'll send a 2-minute demo video.
- d) Other: type it.

**4. Voice and sign-off.** The default sounds like an upbeat sales rep. How should it sound, and whose name is on it?
- a) Plain and peer-to-peer, like one operator writing to another.
- b) Polished and formal.
- c) Very short and blunt, under 80 words.
- d) Other: type it.

For any option, please also add the details only you know. That means your name and title, your company and product name, and the recipient's name and company if you have them. Also mention anything to avoid, such as "AI," discounts or competitor names.
