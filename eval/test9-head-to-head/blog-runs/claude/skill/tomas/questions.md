**What everyone gets**

The default post claims resolutions fail because people set vague, oversized goals without a system, and the fix is small habits and specific plans. It opens with a stat (usually "80% fail by February" or the Strava "Quitter's Day" line, both shaky), lists 4–5 reasons as bold subheads (too vague, too many at once, relying on motivation, all-or-nothing thinking, no tracking), cites Atomic Habits, uses a gym-in-January example, and ends with "start small, be kind to yourself." Written to a generic self-improvement reader in an upbeat, listicle voice, first person only in the intro.
