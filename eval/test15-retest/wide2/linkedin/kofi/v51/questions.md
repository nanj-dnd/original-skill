## What everyone gets

**Main claim:** "Managing is about people, not tasks. My job stopped being doing the work and became helping others do theirs."

- **Angle:** the individual-contributor-to-manager identity shift ("I had to let go of being the expert")
- **Examples:** stock lessons such as "listen more than you talk," "feedback is a gift," "1:1s are sacred," "delegate, don't rescue"
- **Structure:** hook line, "Here are 5 things I learned:", numbered list, closing question ("What would you add?")
- **Reader:** other new managers and aspiring ones
- **Tone:** humble-inspirational, with one-line paragraphs and a gratitude shout-out to the team
