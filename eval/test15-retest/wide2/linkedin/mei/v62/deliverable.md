The most useful thing I did in my first year as a supermarket store manager wasn't a big initiative. It was putting the rota out three weeks ahead instead of one.

Last-minute sick calls went from around six a month to two. Two people on my team of 18 who'd been talking about quitting stayed.

It makes sense when you think about it. If you only know your shifts a week out, you can't book the dentist, sort childcare or say yes to a family thing. Something has to give, and often it's the shift.

There's a lot of talk about management as leadership, vision and culture. Mine has turned out to be mostly logistics. Get the boring things right, and people can actually plan their lives.

If you manage shift workers and want one thing to change, I'd start with how far ahead they know when they're working.
