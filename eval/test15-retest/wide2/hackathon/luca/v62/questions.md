## What everyone gets

**Main claim:** A personal carbon footprint tracker that turns everyday choices into a score and nudges you toward greener habits.
- **Angle:** individual behaviour change, so the user is a consumer logging their own life
- **Examples:** receipt or bank transaction scanning, commute logging, an AI chatbot with "eco tips"
- **Structure:** problem stat, features list, tech stack (React + Firebase + an LLM API), demo
- **Reader:** generic hackathon judges scoring "impact + innovation"
- **Tone:** pitchy and upbeat, with badges, streaks, leaderboards and "small changes add up"

Judges at a climate hackathon usually see several of these, and they look alike.

## A few questions first

Reply in one line, like `1b 2d 3a 4c`, and add free text wherever an option asks for it.

**1. Who is it for?** The default builds for individual consumers tracking their own footprint. Who should the user be instead?
- a) Individuals is fine. Just make it sharper than the usual tracker.
- b) Someone who runs something: a small business, restaurant, farm, building or campus facility
- c) A community or public group: a city office, school, neighbourhood or disaster response
- d) Someone specific you have in mind. Name them.

**2. What can your team actually build in the weekend?** The default assumes a standard web app with an LLM bolted on.
- a) 2 to 3 web or app developers, no special data or hardware skills
- b) We have someone strong in data or ML: maps, satellite data, forecasting
- c) We can do hardware: sensors, a Raspberry Pi or Arduino
- d) Tell me your team size, skills and hours available (for example, "4 people, 2 frontend, 1 Python, 1 designer, ~30 hrs").

**3. What real material do you have?** The default opens with a global CO₂ statistic. A real case and a real number make a stronger pitch.
- a) A local problem someone on the team has seen, like heat, flooding, air quality or water. Tell me where it is and one number if you have it.
- b) Waste or energy use somewhere you know, like a workplace, campus or family business. Tell me what it is and roughly how much.
- c) A required sponsor API, dataset or tech we must use. Name it.
- d) Nothing specific. Pick a case that fits our team.

**4. How will it be judged and demoed?** The default plans a polished 3-minute click-through for generic judges.
- a) A live demo to technical judges, where working code matters most
- b) A pitch to sponsors or investors, where the market and business case matter most
- c) A recorded video or Devpost-style submission
- d) Tell me the time limit and judging criteria, plus anything to avoid (for example, "no crypto" or "must be open source").

Once you answer, I'll give you the angle, the strongest objection with a test you can run on Friday night, and then the idea, the build plan and the demo script.
