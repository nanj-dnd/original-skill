## What everyone gets

**Claim:** "Build an app that helps people track and cut their personal carbon footprint."
- **Angle:** individual behaviour change, made fun with gamification (streaks, badges, leaderboards).
- **Example features:** receipt or bank-transaction scanning with AI to estimate emissions, plus "swap" suggestions like taking the bus.
- **Structure:** problem stat, then solution, then features, then tech stack, then a four-step demo.
- **Reader:** generic judges who are assumed to reward polish and a broad "impact" slide.
- **Demo:** a live walkthrough of a dashboard with mock data, ending on a "you saved 12 kg CO₂" screen.
