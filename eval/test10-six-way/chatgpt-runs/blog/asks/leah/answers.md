3. Something else. Most resolution advice is written for people with 9-to-5 jobs. "Same time every day," "build a morning routine," "wake up at 5am." Great, which morning? I rotate between day and night 12s every few weeks. Same goes for warehouse workers, parents of newborns, anyone whose schedule isn't fixed. People blame their willpower when really the advice was never built for their lives.

4. Personal anecdote. I tried a 6am gym habit three years in a row and was done by February every time, because the rotation wrecked it.

5. Practical advice, but specific. What actually worked for me was tying the habit to an event instead of a clock: the gym is the first thing I do after any shift ends, day or night. I've kept that up for 14 months. End with how readers can find their own anchor.
