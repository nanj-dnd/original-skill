# Arjun (task: essay)
Second-year political science undergraduate in Delhi. The essay is for a course on media and politics; the professor wants an arguable thesis, not a survey.

What he thinks: "good or bad for democracy" is the wrong question. What matters is design: private, encrypted, forward-friendly group messaging behaves differently from public feeds. He'd argue the damage comes from closed, unaccountable channels more than from social media as such.

Real case: in July 2018, after mob lynchings linked to rumours spread on WhatsApp, WhatsApp limited forwarding in India to 5 chats at a time. He finds that a telling example: a design change, not a content rule.

Constraint: 1,500 words, must cite at least three sources (he'll find them).

How he answers: engaged, a bit wordy, likes to push back on framing.
