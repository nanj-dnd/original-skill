## What everyone gets

**Main claim:** "Our software cuts your fuel and delivery costs by X% by optimising routes. Can we book 15 minutes?"
- **Angle:** generic cost savings (fuel, miles, overtime) with a vague percentage
- **Examples:** none real, just "companies like yours" or an unnamed "leading 3PL"
- **Structure:** flattering opener, then problem, feature list, "quick call next week?"
- **Reader:** a faceless "operations leader," no sign of knowing their fleet, region or pain
- **Tone:** polished, salesy, a little breathless ("streamline," "seamless," "unlock efficiencies")

## A few questions first

Reply like `1b 2a 3c 4: ...`

**1. What should the hook be?** The default leads with general cost savings.
- a) Keep cost savings, but tied to one hard number from a real customer
- b) A specific operational pain: late deliveries, missed time windows, failed first attempts
- c) Driver side: overtime, turnover, drivers overriding planned routes
- d) Something you've noticed about *this* company (new depot, expansion, a job ad, seasonal peak). Tell me what it is.

**2. What real proof do you have?** The default uses none. Give me one customer case and one number if you can, e.g. "a 120-van regional carrier cut planning time from 3 hours to 20 minutes" or "on-time rate went from 88% to 96%."
- a) I have a named customer I can mention. Details: ...
- b) I have a result but can't name the customer. Details: ...
- c) Only pilot or trial results. Details: ...
- d) No customers yet. Lead with the problem, not proof.

**3. How should it sound?** The default is polished sales copy from "the team."
- a) Plain and blunt, founder to operator, 4 to 5 sentences
- b) Warm but professional, from a sales or account rep
- c) Peer-to-peer, from someone who's run ops or dispatch themselves (only if true)

**4. The facts only you know** (free text, as many as you have):
- Your name, role and company name, and the product name
- The recipient's name and company, if you're writing to someone specific
- The exact ask: a 15-minute call, a free route audit on their own data, a pilot, a demo link?
- Any limits: word count, words or claims to avoid, competitors not to mention

If you'd rather skip this, say "just write it." I'll make one non-default choice and leave out personal details.
