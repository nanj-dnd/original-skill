**Subject:** How your routes get built

Hi,

Quick question: when one of your drivers finishes a route, how close was it to the one the dispatcher planned that morning?

For most mid-size fleets the honest answer is "not very." Orders come in late, a stop cancels, traffic shifts, and the route gets patched on the fly. Each patch is small. Across a fleet, every day, those patches are where the extra miles and fuel go.

We build route-optimisation software that fixes that: routes are built from the actual constraints (windows, vehicle capacity, driver hours) and rebuilt when the day changes, so the mileage you pay for is the mileage the work needs.

I'm not going to quote you a savings percentage, because it depends entirely on how your fleet runs today. That's what I'd want to find out with you.

Could you spare 15 minutes this week or next? If it turns out there's little to gain, I'll say so.
