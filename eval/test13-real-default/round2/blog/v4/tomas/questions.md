**What everyone gets**
Main claim: resolutions fail because they're vague, too ambitious, and driven by motivation instead of systems.
- Angle: the problem is *how* people set goals (SMART goals, habits, start small).
- Examples: "lose weight", "go to the gym", the crowded gym in January that empties by February.
- Structure: hook about the January rush → 3–4 numbered reasons → "what to do instead" fixes → upbeat close.
- Reader: generic self-improver; tone is friendly listicle, second person, light on the author.
- Stock lines: "it's a marathon, not a sprint", "motivation fades, discipline stays", the oft-cited "80% fail by February" stat.
