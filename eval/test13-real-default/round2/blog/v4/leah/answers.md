1: none of those, really. They fail for a lot of people because the advice is written for 9-to-5 office workers. "Same time every day," "morning routine": great if you have a regular schedule. Useless if you're a nurse on rotating 12s, warehouse staff, or the parent of a newborn. It's not a willpower problem, it's a calendar problem.

2b, plus the one that died, because they're the same story. I tried a 6am gym habit three years running and it was dead by February every time, because my rotation flipped me to nights and 6am stopped meaning anything. What finally worked was anchoring it to an event instead of a clock: the gym is the first thing I do after any shift ends, day or night. I've kept that 14 months now.

3c. Start with the 6am failures, then the switch, then widen it out to everyone else whose life doesn't run on a fixed schedule.

4a, with a bit of bite. My readers are mostly other nurses and healthcare people from my Instagram, so talk to them like a coworker in the break room, not a generic self-improver. And please, no "simply." Nothing about this is simple when you work nights.
