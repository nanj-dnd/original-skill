## What everyone gets

**Main claim:** "An AI-powered personal carbon footprint tracker that nudges people toward greener habits."
- **Angle:** individual consumer behaviour (your choices add up)
- **What's built:** a mobile or web app that logs travel, food and purchases, sometimes by scanning receipts, and converts them to kg CO₂
- **Hook:** gamification with streaks, badges and a friends leaderboard, plus LLM "eco-tips"
- **Data:** generic emissions factors and a mocked user history
- **Demo:** a walkthrough of a fake user's week, ending on a chart showing "you saved 12 kg this month"

Judges see several of these at every climate hackathon.
