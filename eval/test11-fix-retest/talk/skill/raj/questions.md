**What everyone gets**

The choices a pasted prompt makes:
- **Angle**: both-sides "use it wisely" rather than a real position.
- **Examples**: generic (boilerplate, unit tests, a hallucinated API) — nothing from your codebase.
- **Structure**: hook → benefits → risks → three guidelines → "it's a tool, not a replacement."
- **Reader**: assumes a sceptical-but-curious engineering crowd; ignores that an all-hands includes non-engineers.
