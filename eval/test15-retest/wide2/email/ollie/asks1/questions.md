Here are the questions that'll make the biggest difference:

1. **Product & proof:** What's the product called, and what's your strongest concrete result? (e.g., "cut fuel costs 18% for a 200-truck fleet")
2. **Target specifics:** Do you have a specific company/person in mind, or should this be a reusable template? If specific, anything you know about them (fleet size, region, recent news, known pain points)?
3. **Main pain point to lead with:** Fuel costs, late deliveries, driver overtime, dispatcher workload, scaling without hiring, something else?
4. **Differentiator:** What sets you apart from competitors (e.g., real-time re-routing, integrates with their existing TMS, fast setup)?
5. **Call to action:** What do you want them to do: a 15-min call, a demo, a free route analysis or pilot?
6. **Sender:** Who's it from (name, title, company), and is there any mutual connection or referral to mention?
7. **Tone & market:** Formal or conversational? UK or US spelling? (You wrote "optimisation," so I'll assume UK unless you say otherwise.)

If you'd rather not answer all of these, just tell me what you've got and I'll use sensible placeholders for the rest.
