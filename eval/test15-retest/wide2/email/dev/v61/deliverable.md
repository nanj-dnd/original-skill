**Subject:** Route planning: from two hours a day to twenty minutes

Dear {{first_name}},

Route planning is one of the few operating costs that recurs every working day, before a single vehicle leaves the yard.

Customers using our route-optimisation software report that their daily planning has dropped from about two hours to around twenty minutes. Over a five-day week, that returns more than 400 hours a year to the operation. Those hours can go to exceptions, customer queries and the issues that would otherwise wait until the afternoon.

If reducing that overhead at {{company}} is a priority, I would welcome fifteen minutes to show you how the planning works and whether a similar result is realistic for your network.

Would a short call next week be convenient?

Kind regards,

**Before you use it**
- I wrote `{{first_name}}` and `{{company}}` in standard merge-field syntax for a reusable sequence. Match them to your tool's format, and let the tool add your signature.
- Confirm what the 2 hours to 20 minutes figure measures. It could be per planner or per depot, and it could come from a few customers or from most. The "400 hours" line is about 100 minutes saved a day times 250 days, so it only holds if the figure is per planner or per daily plan.
- A named customer behind the figure, with their permission, would strengthen it more than anything else.
