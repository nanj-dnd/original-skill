A few questions so the email lands well:

1. **Product and proof:** What's the software called, and what's the single strongest result you can cite? For example, "cut fuel costs 12% for a 200-truck fleet," a named customer, or a case study.
2. **Recipient:** Do you know their name and company? Is there a reason to contact them now, such as recent expansion, a new hire, rising fuel costs, or a mutual connection?
3. **Their fleet:** Do you know what they run (last-mile delivery, long-haul trucking, mixed fleet)? Roughly how big is "mid-size"?
4. **Main pain point:** Which angle should lead: fuel and mileage costs, on-time delivery rates, dispatcher workload, driver retention, or something else?
5. **Differentiator:** What sets you apart from competitors (e.g., Routific, OptimoRoute, Descartes)? Examples: faster setup, integration with their existing TMS, pricing, or real-time re-routing.
6. **Call to action:** What do you want them to do? Options include a 15-minute call, a demo, a free pilot, or simply replying.
7. **Sender and tone:** Who is it from (founder, sales rep)? Do you want it crisp and formal or more conversational?
8. **Length and extras:** Should I keep it under about 120 words? Do you want subject line options too?

If you don't have answers to some of these, that's fine. Tell me, and I'll use sensible placeholders you can fill in.
