One year as a store manager. Here's the main thing I learned.

Management is mostly logistics, not inspiration.

I spent five years on the shop floor before I got promoted. I thought the rota was admin, something you knock out on a Thursday afternoon.

It isn't. For 18 people, the rota decides whether they can book a dentist appointment, sort out childcare, take a second job, or watch their kid play football on Saturday.

So I started putting it out three weeks ahead instead of one.

Last-minute sick calls went from about six a month to two. Two people who'd been thinking about leaving stayed.

No team-building day. No speech. Just a spreadsheet, finished earlier.

If you manage shift workers, the most motivating thing you can do isn't motivational. It's telling people when they're working far enough ahead that they can plan their lives around it.
