Here are the questions that will shape this most:

**The substance**
1. What are the 2–4 biggest lessons you learned? Rough notes are fine. If you're not sure, tell me what surprised you most, what you'd do differently, and what you got wrong early on.
2. Is there a specific moment or story that captures one of these lessons? For example, a hard conversation, a mistake, or a win. Concrete stories are what make these posts land.

**Context**
3. What was your role before, and what do you manage now? For example, IC engineer to engineering manager of 6, or a team lead promoted from within. Do you want to name your company or industry?
4. Were you promoted internally to manage former peers, or hired in as a new manager? That changes the story a lot.

**Tone and audience**
5. Who do you want reading this? Other new or aspiring managers, your own team, recruiters, or your industry in general?
6. What tone fits you: candid and vulnerable, practical and tactical (a list of takeaways), or reflective and story-driven? Is light humor okay?
7. Is there anything you want to avoid? For example, sounding preachy, the "humbled to announce" style, emojis, hashtags, or being too critical of your company.

**Format**
8. What length do you want: short and punchy (~150 words) or a longer read (~300–400 words)? Do you want a list format or flowing paragraphs?
9. Do you want to thank anyone, such as your team, your manager, or a mentor, or end with a question to drive comments?

If you'd rather not answer them all, questions 1, 2, and 6 matter most.
