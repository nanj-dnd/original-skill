**Subject:** How long does route planning take your team each morning?

Hello,

At many mid-size logistics companies, the day's routes are still built by hand, often by a few experienced dispatchers working against the clock before the first vehicles leave. The plan gets done, but it relies heavily on those people and takes up time they could spend on exceptions, drivers and customers.

Route-optimisation software can take on that first draft. Your planners then review and adjust a plan instead of starting from a blank sheet. That is the problem our software is built to address.

I would welcome a brief call to understand how your team handles planning today and whether this could be useful to you. Would you have 15 minutes in the coming weeks?

Kind regards,
