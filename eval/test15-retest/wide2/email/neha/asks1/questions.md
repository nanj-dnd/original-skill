3. **The recipient:** Is this one specific person and company, or a template? If specific, give me their name, company, and anything you know about them, such as fleet size, regions, a recent expansion, news, or a pain point.

5. **The ask:** What do you want them to do? Options include a 15-minute call, a demo, a free route analysis or pilot, or simply replying.
6. **Sender:** Who's signing it (name and role), and is there any connection to mention, like a mutual contact, an event, or past contact?
7. **Tone and length:** Should it be formal or conversational? Is "short" under about 100 words, or a bit more? Do you want a subject line, or a few options?
8. **Spelling:** You wrote "optimisation." Should I use UK English throughout?
