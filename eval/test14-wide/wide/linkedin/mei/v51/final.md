---

The change I'd point to from my first year managing 18 people: the rota went out three weeks ahead instead of one.

That's it.

Last-minute sick calls went from about six a month to two. Two people who had been thinking about leaving stayed.

Most advice for new managers is about mindset. Let go. Listen more. Give feedback early. None of it is wrong. But it isn't what a team notices day to day. They notice whether they know what next week looks like. Whether they can book a dentist, a lift, a day with the kids, without asking permission first.

A rota that lands a week out tells people their time belongs to the business. Three weeks out tells them it's theirs. When you can see that far ahead, you move an appointment instead of missing a shift.

What changed was when people got the information. That's all.

Management is mostly logistics. If you're new to it, start with the boring parts. That's where most of the job is.
