1: none of those. You're all asking why the resolution fails. Wrong question. Most attempts fail, and that's normal. Count attempts, not outcomes. A failed attempt is data.

2a: Quitting smoking. Took me 4 serious attempts; I've been smoke-free 6 years now. Attempt 2 lasted 5 weeks and broke at a wedding. That's how I learned drinking at weddings was my trigger. Attempt 4 was planned around exactly that. The first three weren't failures. They taught me my triggers: first coffee, driving, my brother.

3a: what to do instead. Treat each attempt like an experiment. When it breaks, write down where and why, then plan the next one around it. And cut "be kind to yourself." My readers include people from a quit-smoking forum and they'll roll their eyes.
