# Why most New Year's resolutions fail (and the one of mine that didn't)

I've made the usual ones. Go to the gym. Fix my diet. Each lasted a few weeks, then quietly stopped, and I didn't even notice the day it stopped.

Then one stuck. Three years ago I started learning Welsh on an app, and I'm now on a 700-day streak. My 12-year-old daughter does it too, and we check each other's streaks at dinner.

I don't think I wanted Welsh more than I wanted to be fit. I think the Welsh resolution was built differently. Here's what I mean.

**1. The ones that fail are outcomes. The one that stuck is a unit.**
"Get fit" and "eat better" describe a state I'd like to be in. There's nothing to do on a Tuesday. Welsh was one lesson, a few minutes, every day. You can't half-do it or over-do it. You open the app, you do the lesson, it's done. The gym never had a version that small.

**2. Nobody was counting.**
The app counts. The number sits on the screen and goes up by one each day, and once it's past a few hundred it is genuinely painful to imagine it going back to zero. My gym resolution had no counter. There was no 23 to protect, so day 24 didn't feel like anything.

**3. Nobody was watching.**
This is the part I underrated. My daughter asks at dinner. Not in a nagging way — she's checking hers too. But it means skipping a day isn't private. The diet was entirely private, which meant quitting it was also private, which is why I can't tell you when it ended.

**4. "A few weeks" is exactly when it happens.**
Both failures lasted about the same length. That's the window where the newness wears off and nothing has replaced it. The streak and the dinner check are what carried Welsh through that window. Willpower didn't come into it, which is good, because I don't think I have more of it now than I did then.

**What I'd do differently**
If I made another resolution, I'd shrink it until it's one small thing I can do daily, I'd put a visible number on it, and I'd tell one person who'll actually ask. Not because any of that is clever, but because it's the only combination that's ever worked for me, and the difference between 700 days and three weeks was never the goal. It was the setup.
