Happy to. A few questions so this sounds like you and not like every other "lessons from year one" post:

**The basics**
1. What's your role and field? (e.g., engineering manager at a startup, team lead in sales, etc.) How big is your team?
2. Were you promoted from within the team you now manage, or did you come in from outside? Were you an individual contributor before?

**The substance**
3. What are the 2–4 biggest lessons you actually learned? Rough notes are fine. Bonus if any of them surprised you or went against what you expected.
4. Is there a specific moment or story that captures one of these lessons? A mistake, a tough conversation, a win, a time something clicked. A concrete story usually beats a list of takeaways.
5. Anything you got wrong early on that you'd be willing to admit publicly?

**Tone and audience**
6. Who do you most want to read this? New managers, your own team, recruiters or your network in general, people considering management?
7. What tone feels like you: reflective and earnest, punchy and direct, a bit self-deprecating or funny?
8. Is there anything to avoid? For example, naming your company, mentioning specific people, or anything that might read awkwardly to your team or boss.

**Format**
9. Length preference: short and punchy (~150 words) or a fuller post (~300–400 words)?
10. Do you want a closing question to drive comments, hashtags, or emojis, or none of those?

Answer whatever you can. Even short answers to #3 and #4 will make the biggest difference.
