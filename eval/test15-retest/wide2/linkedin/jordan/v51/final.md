A year ago I became a marketing team lead, managing three people.

Most posts about a first year in management are built on hard lessons and rough patches. Mine was fine. I still learned a lot, and a fine year is worth writing about, because it's the year most new managers will actually have.

A few things worth knowing if you're leading a small team:

1. With three people, each one is a third of the team. One person's bad week is everyone's bad week, so you either notice it early or notice it late.

2. You'll probably still do some of the hands-on work. Decide which parts on purpose, or the calendar will decide for you.

3. A quiet year is not a sign nothing happened. Steady weeks where the work ships are the result, not the absence of one.

If you're about to step up: you don't need a dramatic story to know you're learning.
