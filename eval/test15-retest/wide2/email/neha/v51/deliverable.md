**Subject:** Late deliveries when the monsoon hits

Hello,

When the rains arrive, a cold-chain route plan that worked in March often stops working. Roads flood, windows slip, and in pharma a late drop can put a whole consignment at risk.

We ran a three-month pilot with a dairy distributor in Pune: 40 vans moving temperature-sensitive stock. Fuel spend fell 14%. Late deliveries went from 1 in 9 to 1 in 25.

Pharma is stricter than dairy, so I won't promise the same numbers. But I'd like 20 minutes to hear how your routes hold up in monsoon and whether Routewise could help.

Would a call next week or the week after work?

Neha
