"Same time every day," "morning routine" — that's impossible if you're on rotating 12s, or a warehouse shift, or up with a newborn. People aren't failing, the advice just doesn't fit their lives. 2a: I tried a 6am gym habit three years in a row and it died by February every single time, because my rotation would flip to nights and 6am meant nothing. What finally worked was tying it to an event instead of a clock: the gym is the first thing I do after any shift ends, day or night. I've kept that going 14 months now.

3a, but make it the anchor-to-an-event idea, not a generic list. And please don't tell anyone to "simply" do anything.
