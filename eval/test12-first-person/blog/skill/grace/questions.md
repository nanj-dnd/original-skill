**What everyone gets**
Main claim: resolutions fail because they're vague, too ambitious, and rely on willpower instead of systems.
Choices a pasted prompt makes:
- Angle: it's a design problem — fix the goal, not the person.
- Examples: gym memberships that lapse by February, "read more", "lose weight".
- Structure: three or four numbered reasons, then a tidy "do this instead" close.
- Reader: anonymous self-improvement crowd; the post never mentions the author's own life.
- Tone: brisk, confident, light coaching voice.

Four questions, all at once. Reply like `1b 2a 3c 4b`, and add a line of your own for 3.

**1. Angle.** The default says resolutions fail because they're badly designed (vague, too big, no system). Which is closer to what you actually think?

**2. Structure.** Default is a numbered list of reasons plus a fix at the end.

**4. Tone.** It's your personal blog with your name on it.
