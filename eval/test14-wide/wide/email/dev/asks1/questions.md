1. **What's your product called, and what's the one-line pitch?** (e.g. "cuts fuel costs by X% by re-sequencing stops in real time")
2. **Do you have a proof point?** A customer name, a hard metric (% fewer miles, hours saved, ROI timeline), or a case study I can reference. Even one number makes a big difference.
3. **Who are you, and how should I sign off?** Name, title, company — and whether this is founder-led or sales-led.
4. **What's the ask?** 15-minute call, a demo, a free route audit using their data, something else?
5. **Do you know anything specific about the target?** Their name, the company, fleet size, type of freight (last-mile, LTL, long-haul), any trigger like a recent expansion or hiring push. Personalisation beats generic every time.
6. **Tone preference?** Casual/direct vs. polished/formal. And UK or US spelling? (You wrote "optimisation", so I'll assume UK unless told otherwise.)
7. **Any constraints?** Word limit, subject line needed, follow-up sequence or just the first touch?
