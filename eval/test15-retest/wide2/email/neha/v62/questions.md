## What everyone gets

**Main claim:** "Our AI-powered route optimisation cuts your fuel costs and delivery times by 15–30%. Worth a 15-minute call?"

- **Angle:** generic efficiency pitch (fuel, time, cost savings) aimed at any logistics company.
- **Examples:** an unnamed "leading logistics company" or a round, unsourced percentage.
- **Structure:** flattering opener about their company, then pain point, product, stat, and a meeting request.
- **Reader:** treats the Head of Ops as a budget holder, not as the person who handles dispatch problems every day.
- **Tone:** polished and salesy ("I hope this finds you well", "streamline", "game-changer").

## A few questions first

Reply like `1b 2a 3c 4b`, and add free text wherever it helps.

**1. What should the email lead with?** The default leads with fuel and time savings.
- a) Keep savings as the lead, but use your real numbers.
- b) A specific operational headache, like late re-routing, failed deliveries, or overtime from bad sequencing.
- c) Something about *their* company: a recent expansion, new depot, or contract you know about.
- d) Something else (tell me).

**2. What real proof do you have?** The default uses an unnamed client and a round percentage.
- a) A named or nameable customer, with a measured result such as "cut miles per drop from X to Y in Z weeks".
- b) A pilot or trial result, even a small one.
- c) No customers yet. We'd lean on a free trial or a quick analysis of their own route data.
- d) Free text with the case and the number.

**3. What exactly are you asking for?** The default asks for a 15-minute call.
- a) A 15-minute call.
- b) An offer to run their last month of routes through the software and send them the difference.
- c) A short demo with their dispatch lead.
- d) Something else (tell me).

**4. How should it sound, and whose name is on it?** The default sounds like a polished vendor.
- a) Plain and direct, founder to operator.
- b) Professional and measured, from a sales or partnerships person.
- c) Very brief, five sentences or fewer, almost like a text.
- Please also add: the **sender's name and role**, the **product or company name**, the **recipient's name and company** if you have them, and anything to avoid (words, claims, competitors).
