## Before the post (step 4)

**Angle:** "Most resolutions fail" counts attempts as if they were outcomes. Most attempts do fail, and that decides very little. What decides it is whether the next attempt is built from what the last one showed you.

**Built from:** 1 (attempts vs outcomes), 2 (a failed attempt is data: coffee, driving, your brother), 3 (the smoking quit, the wedding, attempt 4 planned around it), 4 (an argument with your story as the evidence, for readers who have relapsed), 5 (go after how "failure" is measured, and use the several-attempts number).

**Strongest objection:** "It's all data" can turn into a licence to fail forever. Plenty of people make attempt after attempt and learn nothing, and a forum reader could hear your post as permission for that.
**How the post handles it:** I used this tension rather than smoothing it over. Answer 2 says every failed attempt is data. Answer 5 says no "be kind to yourself" ending. Put together, they give the post its hard edge: an attempt only counts as data if the next one is different. Same plan, same wedding, same drinks is one experiment run four times.
