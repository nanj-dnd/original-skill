1. **Team & skills** — How many people, and what's the mix (frontend, backend, ML/data, hardware, design)? Any stack you want to use or avoid?

2. **Time & judging** — Is it a true 48 hours? What do judges weigh most: technical depth, polish/demo wow-factor, real-world impact, or business viability?

3. **Climate sub-theme** — Is "climate" open-ended, or are there tracks (e.g., adaptation/resilience, emissions tracking, energy, food/ag, consumer behavior, disaster response)? Any sponsor APIs or datasets you're expected/encouraged to use?

4. **Audience for the demo** — Who's watching: technical judges, VCs, non-technical public? Live demo on stage or video submission? How long is the pitch slot?

5. **Ambition level** — Do you want something safe and polished you can definitely finish, or a riskier moonshot that might half-work but stands out?

6. **Hardware/sensors** — Do you have access to any physical kit (Raspberry Pi, Arduino, sensors, drones) or should this be purely software?

7. **Data access** — Comfortable pulling from public sources (NOAA, NASA FIRMS, OpenWeather, EIA, satellite imagery, Google Environmental APIs), or do you want to avoid anything with API-key/rate-limit risk under time pressure?

8. **Prior ideas or preferences** — Anything your team is already excited about or specifically tired of seeing (carbon footprint calculators, etc.)? Any personal angle (a city you live in, an industry you know)?

9. **Scope of "demo plan"** — Do you want a minute-by-minute pitch script, or just the key beats and what to show live vs. mock?
