## What everyone gets

**Main claim:** "An AI-powered personal carbon footprint tracker that gamifies sustainable habits."

- **Angle:** individual behaviour change, measured in kg of CO₂
- **Build:** a mobile/web app that logs travel, food and purchases (often via receipt scan), with an LLM giving tips
- **Hook:** streaks, badges, a friends leaderboard
- **Reader:** generic consumers, pitched to generalist judges
- **Demo:** slides on the problem, then a click-through with mock data, ending on "imagine this at scale"

## A few questions first

**1. Who it serves.** The default targets individuals tracking their own footprint. Who should yours serve?
- a) Individuals (keep the default audience)
- b) A specific group: small businesses, farmers, a city or council office, your campus or employer
- c) A community acting together: a neighbourhood, building, school or club
- d) Someone else (tell me)

**2. What kind of build.** The default is a standalone app with a dashboard. What should you build?
- a) A web or mobile app (default shape)
- b) Hardware or sensors plus data, e.g. energy, air quality or soil
- c) Data or ML on public sources, e.g. grid carbon intensity, satellite imagery, weather or flood data
- d) Something inside a tool people already use: a Slack or WhatsApp bot, a browser extension, a CI/CD plugin

**3. Your team and a real case.** This needs facts only you have:
- a) 2–3 people, mostly web devs, about 24 hours of build time, no sponsor or track rules
- b) 4–5 people with mixed skills including ML or hardware, 36–48 hours
- c) Your own details: team size and skills, hours, judging criteria, any required sponsor API or track, and anything to avoid

Whichever you pick, add **one real problem** you or a teammate has seen, ideally with a number. Examples: "our office leaves 40 monitors on all weekend" or "my uncle lost a crop to a heatwave last year."

**4. Demo and pitch.** The default demo is slides plus a mock-data walkthrough for generalist judges. What should it lean on?
- a) A live demo on real data, built around your case from Q3
- b) A before-and-after story about one person or organisation
- c) Technical depth for engineering-heavy judges
- d) Viability (who pays, how it spreads) for investor- or sponsor-heavy judges
