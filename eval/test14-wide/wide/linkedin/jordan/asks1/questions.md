Happy to help. A few questions so the post sounds like you and not like generic LinkedIn wisdom:

1. **Context**: What kind of team do you manage (engineering, product, sales, etc.), roughly how big, and did you come from an IC role on the same team or somewhere else?

2. **The actual lessons**: What are 2–4 things you genuinely learned? Specific moments are better than principles — e.g., a hard conversation you put off too long, a hire that surprised you, a time you solved a problem you should have let someone else solve.

3. **What surprised you most?** The thing you didn't expect going in often makes the strongest hook.

4. **Tone**: Reflective and candid, upbeat and practical, a bit humorous? Any LinkedIn clichés you want to avoid ("humbled," "journey," emoji bullet lists)?

5. **Length**: Short (under 150 words), medium (200–300), or a longer story-style post?

6. **Audience/goal**: Mostly for other new managers, your own network, or partly to signal something (e.g., you're open to new roles, building a personal brand around leadership)?

7. **Anything to credit or avoid**: Want to shout out your team or a mentor? Anything about your company you shouldn't mention?

Answer whatever you can — even partial answers are fine.
