1. **Your own angle.** Do you have a personal resolution story you'd like in it, such as one you dropped by February or one that actually stuck? Even a sentence or two of real detail will make it feel like yours.

2. **What's your take?** Is there a specific reason you think resolutions fail (goals too vague, relying on willpower, all-or-nothing thinking, the January 1 deadline itself, something else)? Or should I pick the argument?

3. **Voice and tone.** How does your blog usually sound: conversational and funny, reflective, blunt, practical? If you can paste a paragraph from an earlier post, I'll match it.

4. **Who reads it?** Friends and family, a general audience, or a particular group (for example, people interested in fitness, productivity, or parenting)?

5. **How should it end?** On hope with a few practical alternatives, with a challenge to readers, or with the idea of skipping resolutions altogether?

6. **Research.** Do you want stats or studies mentioned (such as how many people abandon resolutions), or should it stay purely personal and opinion-based?

7. **Format.** Plain flowing prose, or subheadings or a short list? And do you want me to suggest a title?
