**Fixed vs open.** The fixed part is the length (~400 words) and any statistics the post uses. I can't search right now, so a figure like "80% of resolutions fail by February" will be marked unchecked if it goes in. Everything else is open: the claim, the angle, the reader, and how it ends.

## What everyone gets

Main claim: resolutions fail because people set them badly. They're too vague, too big, and run on willpower. Make them specific, small and habit-based and they'll stick.

Choices it makes:
- **Thesis**: failure is a technique problem that better goal-setting fixes.
- **Evidence**: opens on the "80% quit by February / only 8% succeed" statistics.
- **Structure**: hook stat, three or four reasons, then a tips list (SMART goals, start small, track habits).
- **Reader**: someone who has already broken a resolution and wants a fix.
- **Voice**: friendly self-help, with no author in it.
- **Leaves out**: the writer's own record, and whether January 1 is even a good time to start.
