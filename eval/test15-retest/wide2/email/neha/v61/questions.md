## What everyone gets

A pasted prompt usually produces an email that says **"Our AI-powered route optimisation cuts your fuel costs and boosts efficiency."** To get there, it makes these choices:
- **Opening:** a flattering line about the company ("I've been following [Company]'s growth…") that leads into a generic pain point.
- **Evidence:** an unnamed "up to 20–30% savings" figure, plus a three-item feature list (real-time traffic, dynamic rerouting, easy integration).
- **Reader:** a generic "Ops leader" who cares mostly about fuel and miles.
- **Tone:** upbeat and salesy, with a subject line like "Cut delivery costs by 25%?"
- **Ask:** "Do you have 15 minutes next week for a quick call?"

## Questions

Reply in one line, like `1a 2c 3b 4a`, and add free text wherever it applies.

**1. What real proof can you put in it?** The default uses a vague "up to 25%" claim.
- a) A named customer result. Tell me who it was, what changed and the number (for example, "Dispatch planning went from 3 hours to 20 minutes at X Freight").
- b) A pilot or trial result we can't name. Give me the type of company and the number.
- c) No customer results yet. Lean on what the product does on their own data instead.
- d) Something else. Type it.

**2. Which problem should it lead with?** The default leads with fuel and mileage cost.
- a) Fuel and mileage cost (keep the default).
- b) Late or failed deliveries and the customer complaints that follow.
- c) Dispatcher hours spent building routes by hand or in spreadsheets.
- d) Driver overtime, unhappy drivers and turnover.

**3. What's the ask?** The default asks for a 15-minute call next week.
- a) A 15-minute call (keep the default).
- b) A free route audit: they send one week of delivery data and you send back what it would have saved.
- c) A low-effort reply, like "Worth a look? Reply yes and I'll send a 2-minute demo."
- d) Something else. Type it.

**4. How should it sound, and who's it from?** The default sounds like a sales rep.
- a) Plain and direct, one operator to another, 80–120 words.
- b) Polished and formal, as if from a sales or business development team.
- c) Warm founder voice: "I built this because…"

Also tell me in free text: your name and title, the product name, and the recipient's name or company if you have one. Add anything to avoid, such as claims you can't back up or competitor names.
