Happy to write this. A few questions first so the talk sounds like you and fits your company:

1. **What's your position?** Do you think engineers *should* use AI assistants for everything, shouldn't, or is it "yes for some things, no for others"? If you're still undecided, is the talk meant to lay out both sides for the company to discuss?

2. **What's your role, and who's in the room?** Are you an engineer, an engineering lead, or someone outside engineering? Is the audience mostly engineers, or the whole company (sales, ops, leadership and so on)? That decides how technical I can get.

3. **What's happening at your company right now?** For example: is there an AI tool policy already, is leadership pushing adoption, are there worries about code quality, security, or IP, or is a decision coming up? Why is this topic on the agenda now?

4. **Do you have your own stories or numbers?** A time AI saved your team a week, a bug it introduced, an incident, a survey of your engineers, before/after metrics. One real example from your company will do more in 5 minutes than any general argument.

5. **What should people do or think afterward?** Change their habits, back a policy, give feedback, or just come away with a clearer picture?

6. **Tone and format:** Is your all-hands formal or casual? Is humor welcome? Will you have slides, or is it just you talking?

If you're short on time, answers to 1, 3 and 4 will help the most.
