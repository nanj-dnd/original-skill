## Opening paragraph

In August, one of our database migrations quietly dropped a NOT NULL constraint. It showed up as a reconciliation mismatch, and it took three weeks to trace that mismatch back to that one change. The migration had been generated by an AI assistant, and the engineer who merged it couldn't tell us what it did. I'm not telling you this to blame anyone. That engineer did exactly what our process let them do. I'm telling you because I was asked to talk about whether engineers should use AI coding assistants for everything, and I think that's the wrong question. My team uses them, and we're going to keep using them. The question that matters is the one everyone outside engineering ends up living with when something breaks: who can explain this change?
