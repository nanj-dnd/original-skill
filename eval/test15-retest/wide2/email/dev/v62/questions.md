## What everyone gets

**Main claim:** "Our AI-powered route optimisation cuts fuel costs and delivery times. Can we book 15 minutes?"

- **Angle:** cost savings, mostly fuel, as the headline benefit.
- **Examples:** a vague stat ("up to 20% reduction in miles driven") with no customer named.
- **Structure:** a pain-point question, then the product, then a bullet list of features, then a request for a call.
- **Reader:** a generic cost-focused executive with no detail about their fleet or region.
- **Tone:** polished and salesy, with "I hope this finds you well," "game-changing," and "streamline."
