## What everyone gets

**Main claim:** "AI coding assistants are powerful but not a silver bullet: use them for boilerplate, tests and docs, keep humans in charge of architecture, security and judgment." A balanced "it depends."

Choices it made:
- **Thesis:** the safe middle (some things yes, some things no), so nobody in the room has to disagree.
- **Angle:** productivity gains vs. risks (bugs, security, hallucinated APIs).
- **Examples:** a borrowed industry stat (e.g., "developers finish tasks X% faster") and generic Copilot/ChatGPT anecdotes, nothing from your company.
- **Structure:** hook stat, pros, cons, "best practices" list, "use it wisely" close.
- **Assumed reader:** engineers, even though an all-hands is mostly not engineers.
- **Leaves out:** what your company actually does or should decide, and who carries the cost (reviewers, juniors, whoever is on call).
