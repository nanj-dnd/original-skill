## What everyone gets

**Main claim:** "Our AI-powered route optimisation cuts your fuel costs and delivery times, so let's talk."

- **Angle:** fuel and mileage savings, with driver shortages and rising costs as the pain.
- **Proof:** a vague "up to 20% reduction" with no customer named.
- **Structure:** a flattering opener ("I noticed [Company] is growing…"), then problem, product, stat, and a request for a 15-minute call.
- **Reader:** a generic "ops leader," not someone who has seen fifty of these emails.
- **Tone:** polished and salesy, with phrases like "streamline," "game-changer" and "I'd love to connect."

## A few questions first

Reply like `1b 2a 3c 4a`, and add any details after.

**1. Which problem should lead?** The default leads with fuel cost savings.
- a) Fuel and mileage cost
- b) Late deliveries and customer complaints
- c) Planner time, meaning dispatchers spend hours building routes by hand each morning
- d) Something specific to your product (tell me what)

**2. What real proof do you have?** The default uses an unnamed "up to 20%."
- a) A customer result with a number. Tell me the type of company, what changed, and by how much.
- b) A pilot or trial result, even a small one. Give me the figure.
- c) No proof yet, so offer to run their own route data and show them the difference.
- d) Nothing I can cite. Keep it to the problem and the offer.

**3. What should the email ask for?** The default asks for a 15-minute call.
- a) A short call
- b) Permission to run last week's routes through the software and send them the results
- c) A one-line reply ("worth a look?")
- d) Watching a 2-minute demo video

**4. How should it sound, and whose name is on it?** The default is polished sales copy from "the team."
- a) Plain and direct, founder to operator, signed with a first name
- b) Professional and formal, from a sales or partnerships lead
- c) Peer to peer, from someone who has worked in operations themselves

**Also useful if you have them:** your name and role, the product name, and the recipient's company or anything you know about it, such as fleet size, region, or a recent expansion.
