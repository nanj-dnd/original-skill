**Subject:** Reducing fuel cost through route planning

Hello,

Fuel is one of the largest costs a fleet carries and one of the hardest to control. Prices move on their own. What an operations team can influence is how many miles each day's routes ask its vehicles to drive.

That is the problem our route-optimisation software addresses. It builds routes that cover the same stops in fewer miles and adjusts them when the day changes: a late order, a cancelled drop, a closed road. Fewer avoidable miles means less fuel bought for the same deliveries.

I would rather not quote a savings figure before understanding your operation, because the answer depends on your network, your fleet and how routes are planned today. A short conversation would let me learn that and tell you honestly whether there is a saving worth pursuing.

Would you be open to a 15-minute call in the next two weeks?

Kind regards,

**Before you use it**
- The email has no names in it. Add the prospect's first name to the greeting, and let your email signature carry your name, title and company. If the prospect's company name is known, "your fleet" can become theirs.
