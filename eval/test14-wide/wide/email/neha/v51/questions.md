**What everyone gets**

Main claim: "We cut fuel and driver costs by 10–20% with AI route optimisation; worth a 15-minute call?"

Choices it makes:
- Angle: cost savings, stated as a percentage range.
- Opening: "I hope this finds you well" or "I noticed [company] is growing," then a flattering line about their fleet.
- Proof: a generic "companies like yours" claim, no named customer.
- Structure: greeting, one problem line, one solution line, one stat, a call-ask, sign-off. About 120 words.
- Tone: polished SaaS-sales voice, mildly formal, ends with "Would you be open to a quick chat next week?"
