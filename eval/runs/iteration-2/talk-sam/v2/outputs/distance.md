## Distance from the default

- **A hard line instead of "use it wisely."** The talk spends its central minute on one non-negotiable rule (no customer data, no production logs, in any external tool) and says it three times. Caused by answer 5 (adopt a concrete norm) and answer 4 (the repeated near-misses).
- **The risk is what goes in, not what comes out.** The default weighed AI output (speed against bugs); the turn of this talk is that output mistakes are recoverable and data leaving the building is not. Caused by the tension between answer 3 (speed vs quality) and answer 4 (data near-misses).
- **Named audience with compliance in the room.** Written for an 80-person tech division, and it hands the line to the risk/compliance people to confirm on the spot. Caused by answer 2.
- **Concrete forms the leak takes** (stack traces with payloads, screenshots, fixtures copied from prod) instead of a generic "be careful with sensitive data." Caused by answer 4.
- **What did not move:** the position on tasks is still the default's middle ("some yes, some no, your judgment"), because that is where you stand (answer 1a). The speed/quality section is close to the default and is deliberately kept short.
