1: none of these quite fits. They fail because the advice is written for people with 9-to-5 lives. "Same time every day," "morning routine," all of it assumes a regular schedule. Shift workers, nurses, warehouse staff, new parents can't do that. Our goals aren't badly designed. The template just doesn't fit our week.

2a: I tried a 6am gym habit three years running and it died by February every time, because my rotation flipped to nights. What finally stuck was anchoring it to an event instead of a time: gym is the first thing after any shift ends, day or night. I've kept it 14 months now.

3c: one suggestion. Anchor to an event, not a clock. My readers are mostly nurses and healthcare people, so make it concrete for them.

4a, with a bit of wry. And please no "simply" anywhere. Nothing about this is simple when you're coming off a 12-hour night.
