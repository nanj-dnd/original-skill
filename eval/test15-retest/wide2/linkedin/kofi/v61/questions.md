## What everyone gets

**Main claim:** "Management isn't about doing the work anymore. It's about helping your team do theirs."

- **Structure:** a hook ("A year ago I became a manager. Here's what nobody told me 👇") followed by a numbered list of 5–7 lessons.
- **Examples:** the stock set of letting go of hands-on work, 1:1s matter, delegation is hard, feedback is a gift, and imposter syndrome.
- **Reader:** other new or aspiring managers in your network.
- **Tone:** humble and inspirational, ending with "What would you add? 👇"
- **Missing:** any real person, moment or number from your year.

## A few questions first

Reply like `1a 2: … 3b 4c`.

**1. Shape.** The default is a list of 5–7 lessons. What do you want instead?
- a) One lesson, told through one real moment (short, about 150–200 words)
- b) Three lessons, each tied to something that actually happened
- c) A mistake I made, and what I do differently now
- d) Keep the list, just make it specific to me

**2. Your real material.** The default has none. Give me one or more of these:
- a) Something that happened, such as a conversation, a bad week, or a decision you'd make differently
- b) A number, such as team size, hires, people promoted, people who left, or hours you used to spend on code or deals versus now
- c) A line someone said to you: your report, your boss, or yourself
- d) Facts I must get right or avoid: your company name (or keep it unnamed), your role, anyone who mustn't be identifiable, and topics that are off limits

**3. Sound and reader.** The default is humble and inspirational for strangers, ending with an engagement question.
- a) Plain and dry, written for peers. No emojis and no closing question.
- b) Warm, with my own team and boss reading it
- c) Candid, more honest about what I got wrong than is usual on LinkedIn
- d) The usual LinkedIn tone is fine, just done well

**4. The lesson itself.** The default is "stop doing, start enabling." Which lesson is actually yours?
- a) That one, because it really was the big shift for me
- b) Something about feedback or hard conversations
- c) Something about how I'm managed, or managing up
- d) Something else (tell me in a line)
