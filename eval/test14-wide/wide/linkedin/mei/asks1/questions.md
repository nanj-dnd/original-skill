Happy to help. A few questions so the post sounds like you and not like generic management advice:

1. **What's your context?** What kind of team do you manage (size, function, industry), and did you move up from an IC role on the same team or come in from outside?

2. **What are the 2–4 actual lessons?** Even rough notes are fine — e.g., "I over-delegated," "1:1s were useless until I changed X," "I didn't realize how lonely it'd be." The more specific and honest, the better the post.

3. **Is there one story or moment** that crystallized something for you? A mistake, a hard conversation, a surprising piece of feedback? One concrete anecdote beats five abstract takeaways.

4. **Tone and length?** Reflective and vulnerable, punchy and practical, a bit humorous? And do you want something short (~100–150 words) or a longer list-style post (~250–350)?

5. **Who's the audience you most want to reach?** Other new managers, your own team/company, recruiters, your network broadly? This changes how much you share and how you frame it.

6. **Anything to avoid?** Clichés you hate, people or situations you don't want hinted at, a company name you'd rather not mention?

7. **Do you want a closing CTA?** (e.g., asking others for their first-year lessons, a thank-you to your team, nothing at all)
