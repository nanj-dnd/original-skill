**Subject:** Routing when your best planner is out

Hello,

In most mid-size operations the day's routes depend on one or two people who know the network by heart. The plan holds until that person is on leave, a driver doesn't turn up, or an extra fifty stops land at eight in the morning, and then someone is rebuilding it by hand while the trucks wait.

We make route-optimisation software for that situation. It builds routes from the constraints themselves, delivery windows, vehicle capacity and driver hours, so the plan on a bad morning is as sound as the plan on a good one, and the knowledge doesn't walk out when your planner does.

I would like fifteen minutes to show you how it works and to hear how you plan today. Would a short call this week or next suit?

Best regards,
