# Your Resolution Didn't Fail. The Advice Did.

Three years in a row, I resolved to hit the gym at 6 a.m. Three years in a row, it was dead by February.

It wasn't laziness. Every time, the rotation flipped me to nights, and 6 a.m. turned into one of two things: the middle of my sleep, or the end of a 12-hour shift. Nobody is deadlifting at the end of a 12-hour shift. I'd miss a week, feel guilty, and quietly let the whole thing go.

Here's what took me way too long to figure out: almost every piece of resolution advice out there is written for people who work 9-to-5. "Same time every day." "Build a morning routine." "Wake up at 6 and work out before the world starts." Great, if your world starts at the same time every day.

Ours doesn't. Nurses, techs, warehouse crews, new parents living on two-hour sleep blocks: we don't have a "morning." We have whatever comes after we wake up. Advice built around the clock was never going to fit a life where the clock keeps moving. So when it falls apart, that's not a willpower problem. It's a fit problem.

What finally worked for me was tying the gym to an event instead of a time. The rule now: the gym is the first thing I do after any shift ends, day or night. Shift's over, gym bag's already in the car, I go. No deciding, no checking what time it is. I've kept it up for 14 months, which is about 13 months longer than any 6 a.m. plan ever lasted.

Psychologists call this an "if-then plan": when X happens, I do Y. It's been tested in dozens of studies, and it reliably helps people follow through, because the cue does the remembering for you. And unlike a clock time, an event shows up no matter which rotation you're on.

So if you're making resolutions this year, try anchoring them to things your shifts already guarantee:

- **End of shift.** The gym, a call to your mom, ten minutes of stretching in the parking lot before you drive.
- **First meal after you wake up.** Whether it's 7 a.m. or 4 p.m., that's when you take your vitamins, drink a full glass of water, or look at the week ahead.
- **The drive home.** Audiobook, language lessons, or that podcast you keep meaning to finish.
- **Last thing before sleep on a work day.** Gym bag packed and in the car, so post-shift you has no way out.

Pick one or two. Hook them to something that's going to happen anyway.

Your resolution was never the problem. It was just written for somebody else's schedule.
