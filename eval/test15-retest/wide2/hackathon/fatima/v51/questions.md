## What everyone gets

**Main claim:** "Build an AI-powered personal carbon footprint tracker that turns everyday choices into climate action."

- **Angle:** individual behaviour change; footprint is the problem, awareness is the fix
- **Examples:** receipt scanning or bank-transaction parsing, food and commute emissions
- **Structure:** problem stat, then app features, then tech stack, then a demo click-through
- **Reader/hook:** generic eco-conscious consumer, gamified with streaks, badges and a leaderboard
- **Tone/demo:** startup-pitch upbeat, with a dashboard on mock data and an LLM "tips" chatbot

## A few questions first

Reply like `1b 2a 3c 4a`, and add any detail in a sentence:

**1. Who is it for?** The default targets individual consumers tracking their own footprint.
- a) Keep it individual, but a specific group (students, renters, commuters in your city)
- b) An organisation's operations: a small business, a campus, an office's facilities team
- c) Adaptation rather than emissions: people dealing with heat, flooding, smoke or drought
- d) A local system: a city council, a farm, a food bank, a transit route

**2. What real material can you build on?** The default runs on invented mock data. Pick any that apply and give the detail:
- a) A real person or organisation with a climate problem you can name or talk to this weekend
- b) A dataset or API you already have access to, or a public one you've used
- c) Your team: size, stack (web, mobile, ML, hardware) and hours you'll actually have
- d) A number the demo could move: kWh, kg CO₂, litres, dollars, minutes, degrees

**3. How should the demo land?** The default is a dashboard click-through with fake numbers.
- a) Live on real data, so judges see an actual result appear
- b) A physical prop or hardware (sensor, Raspberry Pi, printed output)
- c) One user's before-and-after story, told in under three minutes
- d) Judges use it themselves on their own phones during the pitch

**4. How should the pitch sound?** The default sounds like an upbeat startup pitch.
- a) Plain and scrappy: "here's a problem, here's what we built in 48 hours"
- b) Investor-style: market, users, what comes after the hackathon
- c) Technical: the hard part you solved and how
- d) Depends on judging criteria (paste them if you have them)
