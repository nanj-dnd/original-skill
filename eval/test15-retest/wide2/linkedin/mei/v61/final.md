Last-minute sick calls in my store went from about six a month to two this year. The change behind it was small: I started putting the rota out three weeks ahead instead of one.

I manage 18 people in a supermarket. A week's notice sounds fine until you're the one fitting a shift around childcare, a second job, a college timetable or an appointment you booked a month ago. With one week, when something clashes, calling in sick can be the only option left. With three, there's time to swap a shift or ask for a change.

Two people who had been talking about leaving also stayed.

That's the lesson from my first year as a manager I'd pass on. If you run a team on shifts, try putting the rota out earlier and see what happens to your numbers.
