**What everyone gets**

The main claim a pasted prompt produces: "Your drivers are wasting miles and hours; our software cuts fuel and delivery time by 15–20%, want a 15-minute call?"

The choices it makes:
- **Angle**: cost savings, led with a generic pain ("rising fuel costs, tighter margins").
- **Examples**: a vague "companies like yours" or an invented "a client saved 18%".
- **Structure**: flattering opener → pain → three bullet benefits → calendar-link CTA.
- **Reader**: a faceless "Head of Operations" with no reference to their fleet, routes or region.
- **Tone**: polished SaaS-sales ("I'd love to," "streamline," "seamless").

**Questions** (reply like `1b 2a 3c 4b`):

1. The default leads with generic fuel/time savings. What should it lead with instead?

   b) Something you noticed about *this* company (a hiring post, a route expansion, a news item)
   c) A result from an existing customer
   d) Keep it on cost savings

2. The default invents "a client saved 18%". What real material can go in?
   a) A named or described customer with a real number (give me the number: % fewer miles, hours saved, vehicles cut)
   b) A number from your own product (routes planned per day, fleet sizes you handle)
   c) No customer yet — it's an early product; say so honestly
   d) Something else (tell me)

3. The default asks for a 15-minute call via a calendar link. What's the ask?

   b) Send them a sample optimised version of one of their real routes (low-commitment, shows the product)
   c) A reply to one yes/no question
   d) A trial or pilot on a few vehicles
