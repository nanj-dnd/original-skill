Happy to help! A few quick questions so the idea fits your team and the event:

1. **Team:** How many people, and what are your skills (frontend, backend, ML/data, hardware, design)? Any tech stack you prefer or want to avoid?
2. **Time and scope:** Is it a true ~48 hours, or more like 24 hours of real build time? Should the demo be a polished working prototype, or is a convincing mockup acceptable?
3. **Judging:** What are the judging criteria (technical depth, impact, business viability, creativity)? Are there sponsor prizes or required APIs/platforms (for example, cloud credits or a specific dataset)?
4. **Audience and angle:** Do you lean toward consumers (personal behavior change), businesses (emissions tracking, supply chain), communities or cities (heat, flooding, air quality), or climate adaptation vs. mitigation?
5. **Hardware and data:** Is hardware (sensors, Raspberry Pi) an option? Do you already have access to any datasets or APIs (satellite imagery, grid carbon intensity, weather data)?
6. **Ambition:** Would you rather have a safe, very demoable idea or a bolder, riskier one that could win big?
7. **Ideas to avoid:** Are there ideas you've already considered or rejected, or overdone ones (like yet another carbon footprint calculator) you want to skip?
8. **Demo format:** How long is the demo slot (2 min, 5 min?), and is it live on stage, recorded, or at a booth?

Answer whichever ones you can. Even partial answers help, and I'll fill in sensible defaults for the rest.
