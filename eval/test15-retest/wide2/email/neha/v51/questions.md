## What everyone gets

**Main claim:** "Our AI-powered route optimisation cuts fuel costs and delivery times. Can we book 15 minutes?"
- **Angle:** generic efficiency pitch (save fuel, save time, cut costs)
- **Examples:** an unnamed "companies like yours" plus a round claim like "up to 20% savings"
- **Structure:** flattering opener, then problem, features list, and a soft meeting ask
- **Reader:** a generic ops head, with nothing specific to their fleet, region or season
- **Tone:** polished vendor-speak ("I hope this finds you well", "streamline", "leverage")

## A few questions first

Reply like `1b 2a 3c 4a`:

**1. Proof.** The default leans on a vague "up to 20% savings." What real material can you give?
- a) A named customer result with a number (e.g. "cut empty miles 14% across 60 trucks in 3 months"). Paste it.
- b) A result we can share but not name ("a 120-vehicle regional carrier…"). Give the number.
- c) No customer results yet. We'd lead with a free audit or pilot offer instead.
- d) Something else: a pilot figure, a benchmark, or a demo outcome. Paste it.

**2. Hook.** The default opens with a broad "cut fuel and time" pitch. What should the opening hit?
- a) A specific pain this company likely has (late deliveries, driver overtime, peak-season chaos). Name the company or segment if you know it.
- b) Something concrete about them, like recent expansion, new depots or a job posting. Tell me what.
- c) A single sharp number from your proof in Q1, with no lead-in.
- d) Keep the efficiency angle, but make it specific to their fleet type (last-mile, LTL, cold chain, etc.). Say which.

**3. The ask.** The default asks for a 15-minute call. What do you want them to do?
- a) Reply with one detail, like fleet size or depot count, so you can send a tailored estimate
- b) Try a free route analysis on a week of their data
- c) Take a short call or demo
- d) Forward it to whoever owns dispatch

**4. Sound and sender.** The default is polished vendor-speak from an unnamed rep. How should it read?
- a) Plain and direct, founder to operator. Give the name and title it's signed with.
- b) Professional but warm, from a sales or BD rep. Give the name and title.
- c) Technical and numbers-first, for an ops head who distrusts sales pitches.

If you'd rather skip this, say "just write it" and I'll go with a version that stays close to the default.
