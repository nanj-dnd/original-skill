1. **Do you have a story of your own to use?** A resolution you've failed at (or finally kept), and what happened. On a personal blog, one real anecdote usually does more than any statistic. If you'd rather keep yourself out of it, tell me that too.

2. **What's your view on why they fail?** If you already have a theory (goals that are too vague, relying on willpower, the January 1 deadline itself, resolutions being about who we think we should be), tell me and I'll build the post around it. If you don't, I'll pick a clear angle so it isn't just a list of the usual reasons.

3. **What tone do you want?** For example: wry and self-deprecating, warm and encouraging, blunt and contrarian, or thoughtful and reflective. If you can paste a paragraph from an earlier post, I'll match your voice from that.

4. **How should it end?** Options include practical tips for actually sticking with something, an argument for dropping resolutions altogether, or simply a reflection with no advice.

5. **Should it cite research?** Things like the often-quoted failure rates or habit-formation studies. Or should it stay conversational, with no numbers?

If you only answer one, make it #1 or #2. For anything you skip, I'll make a sensible choice and tell you what I chose.
