## Opening paragraph

I was asked to give this talk because, apparently, I have opinions about quality. So let me be honest up front: I don't know whether engineers should use AI coding assistants for everything. My instinct is that they're fine, as long as people are careful. But I want to tell you about a time careful wasn't enough, and how I found out. Last quarter we shipped a checkout form, written with an AI assistant, that had no labels for screen readers. For a blind customer, that meant a page of boxes with no way to tell which one wanted a card number. That customer emailed our support team. It took us two weeks to fix. And I didn't hear about it from engineering. I heard about it from support.
