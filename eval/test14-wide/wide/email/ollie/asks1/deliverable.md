---

**Subject:** Fewer miles per route at [Company]

Hi [First name],

I'm [Your name] at [Product name]. We build route-optimisation software for logistics teams, and ops leads at companies around [Company]'s size tend to be the ones feeling the squeeze from fuel, driver hours, and missed delivery windows.

Our customers typically see [X% fewer miles / Y hours saved per week] within the first few months, without replacing their existing TMS.

Would you be open to a 15-minute call to see whether it's a fit for how [Company] runs its routes? Happy to work around your schedule.

Best,
[Your name]
[Title], [Product name]
[Phone / email]

---

A couple of notes:

- **Fill the bracketed result or cut that line.** A vague "customers see improvements" line is weaker than no claim at all — if you can't get a real number from your team, delete the second paragraph and let the email be three lines shorter.
- **Worth asking internally:** what's the one thing your product does that competitors don't? Even a single phrase ("handles multi-stop time windows", "re-routes mid-shift") in the first paragraph will lift reply rates more than anything else here.
- **Subject line alternatives** if you want a softer open: "Quick question about [Company]'s routing" or "Routing at [Company]".
