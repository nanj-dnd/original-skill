## What everyone gets

**Main claim:** "Social media is a double-edged sword for democracy. It gave ordinary people a voice and new ways to organize, but it also spread misinformation and deepened polarization. Regulation and media literacy are needed to keep the benefits."

- **Angle:** balanced "both sides," with a hedge toward "net worrying."
- **Examples:** Arab Spring, Cambridge Analytica, the 2016 US election, January 6, sometimes #MeToo or BLM.
- **Structure:** intro → benefits → harms → solutions → conclusion.
- **Reader:** a generic instructor; **tone:** formal, neutral, cautious.

If this is for a class, I'll stick to the thesis, angle and outline, and follow any rules your course has on AI use.
