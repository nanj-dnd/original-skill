# Fatima (task: hackathon)
Final-year computer science student in Chennai. Team of 3: she does backend (Python), one designer, one hardware person who brought an Arduino kit and water-level sensors. 36-hour hackathon; judges include an engineer from the city water utility.

What she thinks: most climate hackathon projects are carbon-footprint apps nobody uses. She wants something about adaptation, not just emissions, because flooding is what climate change looks like in Chennai.

Real case: in December 2023 her apartment building's basement car park flooded overnight; about 30 cars were damaged because nobody saw the water rising after midnight.

Constraint: has to demo live in 3 minutes.

How she answers: direct, gives specifics, excited about the hardware.
