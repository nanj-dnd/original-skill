1: none of these quite. The advice is written for people with 9-to-5 lives. "Same time every day," "morning routine": I work rotating 12s, days then nights, switching every few weeks. 2b (with a side of a): I tried a 6am gym habit three years running and was done by February every single time, because the rotation wrecked it. What finally worked was anchoring it to an event instead of a clock: gym is the first thing after any shift ends, day or night. I've kept it 14 months now.
