**Angle**: The thing to sell a head of ops isn't miles saved, it's the two hours a day a dispatcher spends building the plan before any truck moves, and the fact that with the software that becomes 20 minutes.
**Built from**: 1a (lead with manual dispatch as the specific problem), 2a (the 2 hours → 20 minutes figure, which is the only number in the email), 3a (a short call), 4b (plain, from a rep).
**Strongest objection**: A head of ops may see dispatcher time as cheap next to fuel and vehicles; an hour and a half of a planner's day may not feel worth a call. **Test**: send this version and the mileage-led default to ten prospects each and compare replies, or ask two existing customers which of the two they'd have answered.
**Other direction**: Lead with what happens to the plan when the day changes (sick driver, moved window), and position the 20 minutes as the time to rebuild it mid-morning.
