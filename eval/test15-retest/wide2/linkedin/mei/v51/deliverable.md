When I started as a store manager, I put the staff rota out one week ahead. That was a mistake.

Now it goes out three weeks ahead. Last-minute sick calls have dropped from around six a month to two. Two people who had been talking about quitting are still here.

A week's notice looks reasonable from the manager's side. From the other side, it means you can't book an appointment, sort out childcare or make plans with anyone until a few days before. When people can't plan around their shifts, it's usually the shifts that give.

There are 18 people on my team, and every one of them has a life the rota has to fit around.

The lesson from my first year as a manager: before looking at how people turn up, look at how much notice they're given. Often the fix is on my side.
