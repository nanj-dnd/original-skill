The most useful thing I did in my first year managing 18 people was put the rota out three weeks ahead instead of one.

That's it. No framework, no offsite.

Last-minute sick calls went from around six a month to two. Two people who'd been talking about quitting are still here.

I'd have put scheduling near the bottom of what a manager does. It turns out that when people can see their month, they can plan a life around it, and a lot of what looks like a morale problem is really a planning problem.

If you're new to managing a team on a rota: before you work on culture, work on predictability. It's less interesting, and it's most of the job.
