## What everyone gets

**Main claim:** "Managing isn't about doing the work anymore. It's about helping your team do theirs."

- **Angle:** the shift from individual contributor to multiplier ("my job is now your success")
- **Examples:** generic lessons like "listen more than you talk," "1:1s are sacred," "feedback is a gift," "I didn't have all the answers"
- **Structure:** hook line ("A year ago I became a manager. Here's what nobody told me 👇"), then a numbered list of 5 to 7 lessons, then a "What would you add?" close
- **Reader:** other new or aspiring managers
- **Tone:** humble and grateful, ending with a thank-you to the team

## A few questions first
